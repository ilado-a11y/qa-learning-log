## 1. Fundamentals of Testing
### 1.1 Test objectives
    In my own words (IMOW) 

    When the softwaare work product is being tested it is called test objectives.

    The typical test objects are:
    * Evaluating product requirements 
    * Validation whether the product fulfills stakeholders expectations 
    * Verifying whether the product is fulfilling specification requirements
    * Verifying that product is legally and regulatoraly is complied
    * Building confidence around producs's quality
    * Providing stakeholders with information for decision making
    * Causing failures and fixing them 

### 1.2 Testing vs debugging
    IMOW

    Debugging is a procces of reproducing a failure, analysing the defect and fixing it. Testing is triggering failures that are caused by defects. 

### 1.3 Testing vs quality assurance
    IMOW 

    Testing is a producs oriented way of finding defects, while quality assurance is a process oriented focusing on implementation and improving the process. 
### 1.4 Verification vs validation
    IMOW 

    Verification ensures that the product meets it's specification and stakeholders' requirements.
    Validation ensures that the product meets user's needs.

### 1.6 Seven testing principles
| Principle | In my own words | My own example |
1. |---|If the defect wasn't found it doesn't mean that there are no defects|After the testing of a program testers have found that there are 5 bugs. It doesn't mean that there are no bugs, it only means that there are 5 bugs|
2. |---|It is better to start testing early to prevent future failures|Game developers created their own game engine and started to develop their game without testing the engine. In a future it appear that engine have some specific bugs which require to rebuild the defect within the engine, leading to a couple steps back in main development|
3. |---|Appropriate testing. If the produc were tested several times with a same test, showing the same results, it doesn't always mean that the test object does not have a defect. It may require different approaches and test variabnles||
4. |---|context of a testing|there is no single universal test for every test object, each require their own way of testing|
5. |---|Software verification doesn't ensure product's succes, it only shows that the system works the way stakeholdes wanted it to work|---|
6. |defects cluster together|one group of small defects can cause most of the defects which are caused by them|---|
7. |---|it is imposible to test everything|---|
